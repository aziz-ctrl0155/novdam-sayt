Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how Novdam (“we”, novdam.uz) handles data, including data accessed through the “Novdam” application that uses the YouTube API Services and the Instagram API.

1. Who uses the application

The Novdam application is an internal tool used only by the Novdam team to manage Novdam’s own social media accounts: the YouTube channel “Novdam” and the Instagram account @novdam.uz. It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

2. What data we access

  • YouTube: basic channel information (name, ID, subscriber and video counts), the list of our own videos, and YouTube Analytics reports for our own channel (views, watch time, subscribers). We also upload videos to our own channel.
  • Instagram: our own profile information, our own posts and their insights (reach, views, engagement), comments and messages on our own account, and publishing posts to our own account.

We do not access data of any other YouTube channel or Instagram account.

3. How we use the data

Only to publish Novdam content and to see statistics about how our own content performs, so that we can plan better content. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately on the Novdam team’s computer and are never published. Statistics may be saved locally as reports. Data is kept only as long as it is needed for these purposes.

5. Google and YouTube

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Novdam’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

6. Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it does not collect personal data from visitors.

7. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: novdam.uz@gmail.com.
